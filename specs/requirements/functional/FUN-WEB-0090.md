# FUN-WEB-0090

The lead forms the website carries — the quote request and the order flow's invoice step — shall be provided by the envoy web-component widget; the website embeds it and ships no own form backend. **There is no general contact form**: contact is a standing section of static channel rows (DEC-0081).
