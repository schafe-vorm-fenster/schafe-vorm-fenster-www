## Decision

Both unsupported sentences on `/mitmachen` are backed rather than
dropped:

1. **The permanence promise** ("der Dorfkalender bleibt kostenlos", 2022)
   gets a real proof element in `@schafe-vorm-fenster/proof`, with the
   Nordkurier article as its evidence. It is then quotable and its slot
   fills.
2. **The channels are enumerated.** "Six" becomes a list someone stands
   behind, in the hub's messaging, so the page can name them instead of
   counting them vaguely.

## Consequences

- Until both exist, TS-022 D3 forbids the numeral and D7 holds the
  promise back (WEB-F-036). Neither is a copy problem; both are hub
  content tasks.
- Resolves Q-049 and Q-050 as decisions; the artefacts are owed.
