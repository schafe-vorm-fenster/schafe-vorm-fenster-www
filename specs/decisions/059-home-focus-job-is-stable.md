## Decision

The home page's focus job is **"know what is on"**, always. Entry context
changes the **order of the scenes and which CTA is emphasised** — nothing
else.

There was no contradiction to resolve, only an imprecise sentence. Module
one is always the place search (TS-006 D4: the job is fulfilled in place,
never linked), so the page's structure never varies — and reordering plus
emphasis is exactly what principle 6 permits as "selection and order".

## Consequences

- WEB-F-052 keeps its single exception (the empty place calendar).
- The conversion map is right: `/` carries `save-calendar-to-homescreen`.
- The IA's home brief is sharpened from "focus job: set by entry context"
  to "the emphasised offer is set by entry context".
- TS-019 D2's above-the-fold states stand; they are emphasis states, not
  focus-job states. Resolves Q-052.
