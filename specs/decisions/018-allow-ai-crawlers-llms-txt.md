## Decision

AI crawlers are explicitly permitted in robots.txt; the website maintains
an `llms.txt` carrying the short description and core facts, so
LLM-mediated searches ("Veranstaltungskalender Gemeinde") can surface the
product.

## Consequences

Extends the SEO strategy to AI answers. → WEB-F-079.
