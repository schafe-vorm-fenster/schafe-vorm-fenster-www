## Context

SRC-006 is certain about `.de` and `.pl`, unsure about `.at` and the
international domain (".com … or .org").

## Decision

All four are in scope: `schafe-vorm-fenster.de`, `owcezaoknem.pl`,
`schafvormfenster.at`, plus one international domain whose TLD is still
open (Q-001). → WEB-F-060.

## Consequences

Phase 1 builds only `.de` content (DEC-006), but domain wiring and
hreflang must anticipate all four from the start.
