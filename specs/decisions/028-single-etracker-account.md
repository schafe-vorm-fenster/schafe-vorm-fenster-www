## Decision

Website and app report into the same eTracker account, one property for
all domains — cross-domain conversions (homescreen, first event) converge
there. The website measures up to the handover; the app measures
completion. Per-country properties are reconsidered when `.pl`/`.at`
carry real content. The established campaign convention (`etcc_cmp`,
`etcc_med`) continues; entre/svf.li QR redirects already append it.
