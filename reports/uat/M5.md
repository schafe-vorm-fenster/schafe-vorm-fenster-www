# UAT — M5

Persona: first-time visitor with a real goal (Bürgermeisterin looking for a
calendar for her Gemeinde; Vereinsvorstand wanting to publish dates; Landkreis
employee asking for a quote; this time also an English-speaking newcomer).
Target: the M5 preview
`schafe-vorm-fenster-5jupiff6w-schafe-vorm-fenster.vercel.app`. Walked the
five wired conversion goals in German, the first two repeated in English, the
uncovered-place path, and the 404 page with its search, per
`plan/gate-2-scope.md` §2 and this run's instructions. Phone viewport
(360×640) for the first three walks, desktop (1280×800) for the rest.
Screenshots (29) live in the run's scratchpad, not in this repo.

This is a signal report. It records what a naive visitor saw and where she
hesitated — it does not judge pass/fail. Every hesitation noted in
`reports/uat/gate-2.md` was re-walked; see the revisited table below.

## Walk notes

### `/` and `/dein-ort` — "what's on in my village" (DE, phone)

- Landed on `/`. The postcode field ("Deine Postleitzahl") is on the first
  screen, plainly labeled. I typed my real postcode (07743) without
  hesitation.
- This time, typing different postcodes actually produced different answers.
  07743 gave me a fully populated place with dates ("Das ist los in
  Beispielwalde"). A second postcode (10115) gave me a place with no dates
  yet ("In Beispielhausen ist noch nichts eingetragen — du könntest die Erste
  sein.") and the page's focus visibly switched to "publish the first date"
  instead of showing events. Previously every postcode I tried came back with
  a confident, fully-populated demo answer — this time the two answers were
  genuinely different, which is the first time I could tell the search was
  actually doing something with what I typed.
- On both result pages, scrolling down past the first screen of content
  (the postcode search, the headline, and the date list or "be the first"
  card) lands on a long stretch of blank white space — roughly a full
  screen's height or more — before the footer starts. Nothing indicates
  the page is still loading; it just looks unfinished or broken past that
  point, on both phone and desktop, in German and in English.
- The nav item is still labeled "WAS IST LOS" — nothing in the wording says
  "search for your town here."
- Clicking "Kalender von Beispielwalde auf den Homescreen legen" pointed to
  `app.schafe-vorm-fenster.de/beispielwalde` — correct handover target.
- A small badge reading "NICHT MOTIVGENAU · PLATZHALTER" sits over the hero
  image on this and several other pages, and faint text ("Ortsansicht") is
  visible through the darkened photo behind the headline — as a visitor I
  would not know what either of these labels means.

### 404 page — "the wrong address" (desktop)

- Visited a made-up path. This time I landed on a real page: "Seite nicht
  gefunden — Diese Adresse gibt es nicht. Gib deine Postleitzahl ein, dann
  zeigen wir dir, was in deinem Ort los ist." — with an actual postcode
  field and search button, not a placeholder note.
- Typing my postcode and searching took me straight to
  `/dein-ort?ort=07743` with real results — the search on the 404 page
  works exactly like the one on the homepage.
- "Zur Startseite" also works as a fallback if I don't want to search.

### Uncovered place — "my postcode doesn't exist yet" (desktop)

- Searched a postcode the demo data doesn't cover (99999). Landed on
  `/dein-ort/starten?ort=99999`, the postcode staying in the URL as a query
  parameter, not becoming part of the path.
- The page told me plainly: "99999 steht noch nicht im Dorfkalender. Das
  lässt sich ändern — mit einem WhatsApp-Foto vom nächsten Flyer." — and
  offered "99999 eintragen", a link that goes straight into the
  registration flow with my postcode already carried over.
- It also showed a real nearby example ("Beispielgemeinde Musterdorf") with
  actual dates, so I could see what the outcome looks like once someone
  does this for my area.
- Same blank-space gap as on the covered-place pages: a large empty area
  between the green "Was es braucht…" band and the footer, where an
  example events section is present in the page's text but not visible on
  screen.

### `/mitmachen` → `/mitmachen/registrieren` — "I want to publish our dates" (DE, phone)

- "TERMINE VERÖFFENTLICHEN" in the nav led me straight to `/mitmachen`.
  "Kostenlos anmelden" is the one clear next step.
- Step 1 asks for a postcode, same field as the homepage.
- Step 2's question group is still labeled "Wer veröffentlicht die Termine?
  DEMO-DATEN" — answering a question about my own, real club under a label
  that says "demo data" still gave me pause.
- Step 3 (how the dates reach them) no longer carries that label.
- The footer contact form's note now reads "DEMO-DATEN — Es wird nichts
  verschickt — der Versand ist noch nicht angeschlossen." — the internal
  ticket number that used to sit there is gone; this reads like a plain
  explanation now.
- Reached "Fast geschafft" / "Weiter in der App" at the end, handing off to
  `app.schafe-vorm-fenster.de/registrieren`.

### `/dein-kalender` and `/deine-region` — "we want a briefing" (DE, phone)

- The heading "Heute gegen mit dem Produkt" still reads like a dropped word
  — I paused on it the same way as before.
- "Beratungstermin buchen" / "Termin für ein Kennenlerngespräch buchen" is
  offered on both pages, each labeled "(öffnet neuen Tab) · Daten gehen an
  Google". This time, following the link actually opens a working Google
  Calendar booking page with a real time-slot picker — last time every one
  of these links was dead.
- On `/deine-region`, "Beispiele aus dem Landkreis deiner Region" now shows
  a list of real-looking place names (Beispielgemeinde Musterdorf,
  Beispielort Musterhagen, Musterbach, Beispielwalde, Musterfelde,
  Beispielhof Musterheide) — the raw internal identifier I saw last time is
  gone.

### `/deine-region/angebot` — "we want a quote" (desktop)

- Reached via "Angebot anfragen". The form (Organisation, Name, E-Mail,
  Telefon, Worum geht es?) still carries a "DEMO-DATEN" label directly
  above the fields.
- Filling in all fields and submitting immediately produced a different,
  slightly alarming message: "Das ging sehr schnell. Sieh die Angaben noch
  einmal durch und schick sie dann ab." — as if the form suspected I was a
  robot for moving too fast. Submitting again after actually pausing a few
  seconds (as a person reading their own answers back over would) produced
  the real result: "Danke — deine Anfrage ist angekommen. Das ist die
  Demo-Fassung des Formulars: Es wurde nichts verschickt und nichts
  gespeichert. Im fertigen Dorfkalender meldet sich ein Mensch bei dir." —
  a clear, honest confirmation. Last time, nothing told me the request had
  gone anywhere at all.

### `/dein-kalender/bestellen` — "we want to buy a calendar" (desktop)

- Step 1 asks for a postcode; searching surfaced a "+ Beispielwalde" link
  as a separate click from the search itself — the same two-step pattern
  as before (search, then add).
- After adding one place, the counter still reads "1 Orte ausgewählt"
  (singular "1 Ort" would read more naturally).
- Step 3 ("Wohin geht die Rechnung?") now shows one clear call to action
  ("Weiter") for the step itself; the "Absenden" button visible further
  down belongs to the unrelated footer contact form, not to this step —
  clicking "Weiter" is now the only way to proceed here, which resolves
  last time's confusion about two competing actions.
- However: clicking "Weiter" changes its own label to "Weiter · Moment …"
  and nothing else happens for a few seconds before the page moves to step
  4 — no separate spinner or progress indicator elsewhere on the page, so
  in that pause I could not tell if my click had registered.
- I clicked "Weiter" without filling in any of the invoice fields
  (organisation, contact, email, address are all blank) and the flow let
  me through to step 4 anyway — nothing marked the fields as required or
  stopped me.
- Step 4 shows the embed snippet. It still reads
  `data-portalize-organizer-id="demo-organizer-bestellen"` — nothing on the
  page signals that this particular code is a placeholder rather than my
  own calendar's.

### `/en/take-part` → `/en/take-part/register` — English repeat (desktop)
