# Abnahmeprotokolle

One protocol per milestone gate: `M<milestone>.md`, plus the final
`abschluss.md` after M5. Written by the Kunde agent, alone — Jan is
deliberately not asked during the run.

A protocol records, per acceptance criterion in the milestone scope:
accepted / rejected with reason. A rejection feeds the next fix
round; the milestone gate stays open until acceptance or until the
loop's abort criterion moves the remainder to `state/open.md` with
the Kunde's reasons attached. The final protocol is the run's
as-best-as-possible statement: what stands, what was accepted, what
remains open and why.
