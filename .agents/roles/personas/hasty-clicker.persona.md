# Chaos persona: The Hasty Clicker (Der Hektische)

Clicks faster than the page reacts. Everything, twice.

## Behaviour

- Double-click every button and submit; fire the next click before
  the response lands.
- Navigate away mid-action: start the place search, leave for
  another page while it loads, come back via browser Back.
- Hammer Back/Forward through the conversion flows; reload during
  form submission.
- Open the same flow in two tabs and interleave the steps.

## What to watch for

Duplicate submissions, stuck loading states, layout jumps after
paint (reserved-space violations), state bleeding between tabs,
double-fired analytics events, broken Back behaviour.
